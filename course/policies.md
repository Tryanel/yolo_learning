# Course Policies

## What Counts as Completion

A lab is complete when:

- The required submission files exist.
- `python tools/course.py grade labXX` reports a passing score.
- You can explain the key decision you made in the lab.
- `time.txt` records the number of hours spent.

## Data Policy

Do not commit large datasets, videos, or model weights unless the course explicitly asks for a tiny sample. Keep heavy files in ignored folders such as `data/raw/`, `outputs/`, or cloud storage.

## Model Policy

This course prefers small models and reproducible experiments. Start with nano models and small image sizes. Move training to Colab or another cloud GPU when local hardware is weak.

## Academic Honesty

You may use docs, examples, AI assistance, and public tutorials. Your final lab answers must still be your own: describe what you ran, what happened, what failed, and what you would change next.

## Licensing Note

Ultralytics tooling and model families may have license terms that matter for commercial use. Learning and experimentation are the focus here. Before commercial deployment, check the current upstream license and platform terms.
